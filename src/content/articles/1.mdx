# The Physics of Software Architecture

When you build small applications, you are playing with legos. When you build large-scale distributed systems, you are doing civil engineering. The rules of physics begin to apply to your data.

## Data Gravity

Data gravity is the concept that as data accumulates, it builds mass. This mass attracts services, applications, and other data to it. You cannot simply "move" a petabyte database without massive gravitational friction.

> "Software architecture is the art of drawing lines that I call boundaries. Those boundaries separate software elements from one another, and restrict those on one side from knowing about those on the other." — Robert C. Martin

### Managing Entropy

Every new feature increases the entropy (disorder) of a system. To maintain a brutalist, clean architecture, we must actively inject energy (refactoring) to reduce entropy. 

If we don't, the system collapses under its own weight.
